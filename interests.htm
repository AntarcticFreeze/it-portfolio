My Interests

Outside of my coursework, I like spending time on hobbies that allow me to relax or learn something new. My interests range from reading and language learning to video games. Each of them gives me something to look forward to in my free time.

Reading

Reading is one of my favorite ways to spend my free time. I'm primarily interested in historical and horror books, and I enjoy both fiction and nonfiction. When reading about history, I'm especially drawn to military history and events from the twentieth century. Sometimes I read simply to get immersed in an interesting story, while other times I want to learn more about a particular subject that catches my attention. I like that reading can be both entertaining and educational, depending on what I'm in the mood for.

Learning Foreign Languages

I enjoy studying foreign languages and learning about the cultures associated with them. I am currently learning German. My interest in it initially came from listening to German music. I became curious about the language and wanted to understand more of what I was hearing. As I started studying German, I also developed an interest in German-speaking countries and their cultures. Since then, I've been gradually building my vocabulary and grammar skills. Some parts, such as the grammatical genders and cases, are quite challenging, but I enjoy making progress and becoming more proficient over time. I have taken Spanish classes before, too, and would like to eventually explore other languages as well.

Video Games

Video games are one of my favorite ways to relax and spend my free time. I enjoy a variety of genres, although first-person shooters are among my favorites. Games like Enlisted and the Battlefield series appeal to me because of their competitive gameplay and opportunities to use different strategies. I also enjoy exploring the open worlds of Grand Theft Auto V and Red Dead Redemption 2, especially when I want a more relaxed experience. Garry's Mod is another favorite because of the creative freedom it offers via sandbox gameplay and community-created content. I appreciate the variety that gaming offers, whether I'm looking for a challenge or just want to have fun.
